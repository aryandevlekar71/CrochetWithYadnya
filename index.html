About Yadnya

Kindness in Every Knot

Hi, I'm Yadnya! Crochet is my language of love. I started this little shop because I believe handmade things carry something special — a piece of the maker's heart.

Every plushie, keychain, and hair tie I make is stitched with patience, care, and genuine warmth. My mission is simple: to share kindness and love, one crochet at a time.
